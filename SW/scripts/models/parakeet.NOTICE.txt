Parakeet speech models

Parakeet TDT 0.6B v2 and Parakeet TDT-CTC 110M were created by NVIDIA and are
licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence
(CC-BY-4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The files here are the int8 ONNX conversions published by the sherpa-onnx
project (k2-fsa, https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx), copied unchanged.
OASIS uses them to turn speech into text on this PC.
