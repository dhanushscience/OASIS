# Code of Conduct

We want OASIS to be a friendly place for everyone, in the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent.
- Respect different views, skills and backgrounds.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person, and take feedback with grace.
- Keep it on topic. No harassment, insults, discrimination or sharing of other people's private details.

## What happens if someone breaks this

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests, and may block people who keep causing harm.

## Reporting

If something is wrong, use the private **Report a vulnerability** form on the Security tab and say it is a conduct report, or message a maintainer directly. We will keep it private.

This applies in issues, pull requests and any space that represents the project.
